I'm a robotics and autonomous systems engineer focused on building reliable, real-world driving intelligence. Currently, I'm pursuing a Master's in ECE (Robotics track) at the University of Michigan, working at MCity and UMTRI on autonomy, simulation, and planning for self-driving vehicles. My background spans electrical engineering, machine learning, and robotics, with hands-on experience across perception, planning, and control systems.

My work covers the full autonomy stack: I've developed ROS2 C++ planning modules in Autoware, validated through CARLA and VI-grade simulation, built synthetic data pipelines using Parallel Domain to reduce sim-to-real gap, and worked on sensor calibration and embedded deployment. On the research side, I focus on efficiency-aware autonomy and learning-based planning, including Vision-Language-Action models for driving, token-efficient techniques integrated into planners, and transformer-based end-to-end driving systems evaluated on benchmarks like Bench2Drive and NAVSIM.
